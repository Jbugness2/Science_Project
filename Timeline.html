Chronology

1750

Born March 16 in Hanover, Germany.

1772

Moves to Bath, England to keep house for her brother William.

1783

Begins independent sky sweeps; discovers three new nebulae.

1786

Discovers her first comet — the first woman ever to do so.

1787

Awarded an annual salary of £50 by King George III — first paid female scientist in Britain.

1797

Presents a catalogue of 561 missing stars from Flamsteed's atlas to the Royal Society.

1822

Returns to Hanover after William's death.

1828

Awarded the Gold Medal of the Royal Astronomical Society.

1838

Elected honorary member of the Royal Astronomical Society.

1846

Receives the Gold Medal for Science from the King of Prussia on her 96th birthday.

1848

Passes away January 9 in Hanover, aged 97.
