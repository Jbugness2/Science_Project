Caroline Herschel

1750  ·  1848  ·  Astronomer  ·  Pioneer
◆Timeline of Life & Discoveries◆
1750Born March 16 in Hanover, Germany.
1772Moves to Bath, England to keep house for her brother William.
1783Begins independent sky sweeps; discovers three new nebulae.
1786Discovers her first comet — the first woman ever to do so.
1787Awarded an annual salary of £50 by King George III — first paid female scientist in Britain.
1797Presents a catalogue of 561 missing stars from Flamsteed's atlas to the Royal Society.
1822Returns to Hanover after William's death.
1828Awarded the Gold Medal of the Royal Astronomical Society.
1838Elected honorary member of the Royal Astronomical Society.
1846Receives the Gold Medal for Science from the King of Prussia on her 96th birthday.
1848Passes away January 9 in Hanover, aged 97.
◆Orbital Mechanics & Kepler's Third Law◆
T² = a³
Kepler's Third Law  ·  units: AU & years
T = Orbital period in Earth years
a = Semi-major axis in Astronomical Units
1 AU = 149.6 million km (Earth–Sun distance)
Worked Example — 35P/Herschel-Rigollet
Step 1 a = 57.6 AU
Step 2 a³ = 57.6³ = 190,955
Step 3 T² = a³ = 190,955
Step 4 T = √190,955 ≈ 437 years

→ The accepted orbital period of 35P/Herschel-Rigollet is ~155 years, confirming an elliptical orbit.
