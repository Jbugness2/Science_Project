Timeline

1750

Born in Hanover, Germany, the eighth child of Isaac and Anna Herschel.

1772

Moves to Bath, England to keep house for her brother William.

1783

Begins independent sweeping of the heavens; discovers three nebulae.

1786

Discovers her first comet — becoming the first woman to do so.

1787

Awarded an annual salary of £50 by King George III — first paid female scientist in Britain.

1828

Awarded the Gold Medal of the Royal Astronomical Society.

1848

Passes away in Hanover, aged 97.
