Introduction

This site explores the life and legacy of Caroline Herschel, a German-British astronomer who became the first woman to discover a comet and the first woman in Britain to receive a salary for scientific work.

Use the navigation above to explore her biography, discoveries, timeline, and lasting legacy. Sources are on the final page.
